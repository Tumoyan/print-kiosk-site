Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your documents and data when you use Print Kiosk self-service printing kiosks (“the kiosk”, “we”). The kiosks are operated by iTpich. Contact: itpichinfo@gmail.com, +374 44 150 273, +374 43 092 020.

1. Documents you print

  • When you print from a USB drive, the selected file is copied to the kiosk only for your order. The kiosk reads the drive and never writes to it.
  • When you print by email, the attachment from your message is downloaded to the kiosk only for your order.
  • When you make a copy, the scanned pages are stored on the kiosk only for your order.
  • After your order is printed, its files are deleted from the kiosk. If printing fails (for example, the printer runs out of paper), the files are kept for up to 7 days so the order can be resolved, and are then deleted automatically.
  • We do not read, analyze, copy or use the content of your documents for any other purpose.

2. Printing by email and Gmail access

Email printing uses a Gmail mailbox owned by the kiosk operator. The kiosk software accesses only this mailbox through the Gmail API with the gmail.modify permission. This permission is used only to read messages sent to the kiosk, to send scans you asked to receive by email (section 3), and to move processed messages to the mailbox trash; the software does not change other messages. It never accesses your own email account.

  • When you start email printing, the kiosk shows a one-time address with a short code. The software looks only for messages that contain this code in the recipient address or subject and that arrived after you started; it shows you only the attachments of those messages.
  • From such messages it reads the sender address (shown on the screen partially masked), the time received, and the attachments. Message text is not used. The sender address is not stored anywhere and is never added to our newsletter list.
  • After printing, messages stay in the operator's mailbox for at most 7 days and are then moved to the trash; Gmail permanently deletes messages from the trash after 30 days.

Print Kiosk’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Gmail is used only to show and print the documents you sent to the kiosk; it is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not shared with anyone except as needed to provide printing.

3. Scan to email

If you choose to receive a scan by email, you type your email address on the kiosk screen. We use it to send you the PDF from the operator’s Gmail mailbox; the sent copy is moved to the mailbox trash right after sending. The address is kept only in the kiosk’s memory until the email is sent and is not stored, unless you leave the newsletter box ticked (section 6).

4. Payment

Payment is made by card through the bank’s payment terminal at the kiosk. Card data is processed by the bank; the kiosk does not see or store card numbers. We store the order number, amount, payment status and the bank’s transaction reference.

5. Contact requests

If you leave a request on the kiosk screen (“Contact us”, or your phone number for a refund), we store your name, phone number and, if you entered it, your email address, together with your order number if any. We use this data only to answer your request or return your money, with your consent given on the screen. These requests are kept for up to 1 year and then deleted. Your email address is added to our newsletter list only if you leave the newsletter box ticked (section 6).

6. News and offers (newsletter)

Where you type your email address on the kiosk — “Scan to email” and a contact request — the screen shows a separate box “Send me news and offers — unsubscribe at any time”. The box is ticked by default; you can untick it before you pay or send the request. Printing, sending your scan and answering your request do not depend on this box.

  • If the box stays ticked, we store your email address, the date and time, the kiosk, where you gave consent (scan or request), the language of the screen and the version of the consent text, and later dates when you used the same address again.
  • We use this address only to send you news and offers about the iTpich printing service, on the basis of your consent. We send these emails manually from itpichinfo@gmail.com (Gmail, a Google service).
  • Every newsletter tells you how to unsubscribe. You can withdraw your consent at any time by replying “Unsubscribe” or by writing to itpichinfo@gmail.com; we will stop sending you newsletters. After that we keep only a note that the address unsubscribed, so that ticking the box again at a kiosk does not subscribe you again. If you ask us to delete your address completely, we will delete it.
  • We keep the address until you withdraw your consent or ask us to delete it. Addresses of people who send documents for email printing (section 2) are never added to this list.

7. Technical data

The kiosk records technical events needed to operate and fix it: order numbers, page counts, prices, printer and payment statuses, and error messages. These records do not contain document contents, names, phone numbers or email addresses. Staff notifications about problems (for example, “printer out of paper”) also contain no personal data.

8. Who has access

Only the kiosk operator’s staff can access order records, contact requests and the newsletter list, through a password-protected administration panel. We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it with third parties, except the bank that processes your payment, Google (Gmail), through which scans and newsletters are sent, and service providers that host our servers.

9. Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent (including the newsletter consent), by writing to itpichinfo@gmail.com. Please include your order or request number if you have one.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with its effective date.
