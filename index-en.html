iTpich — self-service printing kiosks

Print from a USB drive or email, make copies and send scans to your email. No staff and no account needed — pay by card on the spot.

Services

  • From a USB drive

    Insert your USB drive into the kiosk port and choose a file.

  • From email

    Send the file from your phone to the address shown on the kiosk screen — it appears within seconds.

  • Copy (Xerox)

    Place a paper document on the scanner and get a copy.

  • Scan to email

    Scan a document and receive the PDF in your inbox.

How it works

  1. Choose where your document is: USB drive, email or scanner.
  2. Choose pages, number of copies and one- or two-sided printing. You see the price before you pay.
  3. Pay by card at the kiosk terminal and take your pages.
