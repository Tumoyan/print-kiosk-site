Print Kiosk

Self-service kiosks for printing documents. No staff and no account needed — pay by card at the kiosk and take your printout.

How it works

  1. Bring your document on a USB drive, send it by email to the address shown on the kiosk screen, or copy a paper document on the kiosk scanner.
  2. Choose pages and options — copies, one- or two-sided printing. The price is shown before you pay.
  3. Pay by card at the kiosk terminal and take your pages.

Printing by email

The kiosk shows a personal one-time address with a short code (for example …+K7Q2@gmail.com). Send your file there as an attachment and it appears on the kiosk screen within seconds. The kiosk only sees messages that contain your code and arrived after you started — never anyone else's.

Your documents

Files are used only to print your order and are deleted from the kiosk after printing. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
